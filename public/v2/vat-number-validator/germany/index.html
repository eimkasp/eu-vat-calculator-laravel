European Union guidance

Germany VAT number format

Prefix
DE
Structure
DE followed by the national identifier

The national part is a country-specific sequence of digits and, for some countries, letters.

Only national tax administrations issue VAT identification numbers. VIES confirms registration status but is not tax or legal advice.

European Commission guidance on VAT identification numbers

VAT validation in Germany

Germany VAT numbers use the prefix DE followed by the registration number. You can enter the full number with or without the prefix — our tool detects the country automatically.

Germany applies a standard VAT rate of 19%. Use our Germany VAT calculator to calculate VAT amounts for Germany.

Frequently asked questions

                    

                                                    

                                
                                    What is a VAT number?
                                    
                                
                                
A VAT identification number identifies a business registered for VAT in an EU member state. It is required for intra-community supplies and B2B invoicing across the EU.

                            

                                                    

                                
                                    How does VIES validation work?
                                    
                                
                                
VIES forwards your query to the national VAT database of the member state that issued the number and returns its registration status, together with the company name and address where the member state shares them.

                            

                                                    

                                
                                    Why should I validate VAT numbers?
                                    
                                
                                
Checking a customer's VAT number before you invoice lets you apply the reverse charge or zero-rate an intra-community supply with confidence, and gives you an audit trail for your tax authority.

                            

                                                    

                                
                                    How current are the results?
                                    
                                
                                
Results come from national tax administrations through VIES. A result is reused for up to 7 days to keep lookups fast, and the data source is shown with every result.

                            

                                                    

                                
                                    Can I validate VAT numbers through an API?
                                    
                                
                                
Yes. The free REST API supports single and batch validation. See the API section on this page for the endpoint and a live example.

                            

                                            

                


                
Validation results come from the European Commission VIES database. Results are cached for speed, so confirm critical decisions with your national tax administration.
